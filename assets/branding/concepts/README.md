## 01 — observation

Artwork: `01-observation.png`

### Prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Design one outstanding professional logo concept for Geis, a desktop protein structure creation and exploration workbench named after scientific illustrator Irving Geis. It connects prediction, visualization, a node graph, molecular inspection, and publication image creation. This is a BRAND SYMBOL, not a scientific illustration.
Presentation: square canvas, uniform ink navy #0b0d12 background, one centered flat pale blue #6cc2ff symbol occupying 48 percent of canvas width, exact word "Geis" below in carefully kerned medium modern sans serif in off-white #e6e9f2. Generous whitespace. Logo symbol must work in one ink at 24px. No gradients, lighting, textures, 3D renders, shadows, mockups, secondary words, numbering, or extra examples. No generic DNA, atom orbitals, realistic protein diagrams, ribbon-letter G, recycling mark, or three-lobed AI knot. Concept: SECTION / REVEAL. A bold irregular rounded molecular silhouette, a compact softly faceted pebble-like shape with a deliberate diagonal slice displaced very slightly to expose a precisely designed internal negative-space cavity. The cavity is a simple angular folded channel, suggesting a structure revealed by looking inside. Maximum two flat filled pieces. Strong silhouette, editorial, unexpected. Do not draw an eye, lens, magnifying glass, sphere or cube. Think scientific cutaway distilled to an iconic graphic glyph.
