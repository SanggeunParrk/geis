## Prompt

Use case: logo-brand.
Create a distinctive minimalist LOGO SYMBOL abstracting MYOGLOBIN, the small globular heme protein, for a scientific visualization workbench named after Irving Geis. Symbol only: absolutely NO text, NO word Geis, NO lettering, no labels.
Design concept: the characteristic asymmetric compact globin fold cradling its heme. Distill the surrounding alpha-helical bundle into FOUR short broad curved cobalt-blue graphic bands, packed at different oblique angles into a softly irregular globular silhouette. Each band has ONE simple negative-space cut or fold to imply a helix, not many corkscrew turns. Their arrangement must read as a compact folded protein rather than radial petals. One long curved band along the left edge, a shorter diagonal cap near the top, a folded returning segment to the right, a lower crossing segment wrapping beneath. Clear intentional unequal lengths and asymmetric negative spaces, no rotational symmetry. A single small warm coral-orange flat lozenge, suggesting the heme plane seen obliquely, nests OFF CENTER in a pocket toward the right, partly enclosed by the blue forms. Heme is only 12 percent of the total emblem width, not a large diamond or glowing central bead. The blue bands feel structurally interrelated as one folded chain. This is a deliberately simplified graphic symbol informed by the globin fold, not a claim of an accurate molecular model.
Style: excellent contemporary graphic design, reductive flat vector-like shapes, very clean precise edges, disciplined thick forms, memorable silhouette that works at 32 pixels. Maximum five colored graphic pieces, generous negative spaces. Solid pale cobalt / clear sky blue #6cc2ff and one muted warm coral accent #ef875f, on uniform deep ink navy #0b0d12. NO shading, gradients, glow, shadows, material texture, 3D rendering, photorealism. No realistic molecular diagram, no spaghetti, no atomic balls, no bonds, no literal ring chemistry.
Composition: one symbol centered on square canvas, emblem occupies about 58 percent canvas width, abundant whitespace. No external tile, no mockup, no border, no presentation board, no extra variants. Avoid generic flower, three-blade swirl, atom orbitals, DNA, recycling mark, eye, monogram, ribbon letter G, decorative arrow. The final must feel like a thoughtful MYOGLOBIN-derived brand mark, not an illustration of a whole protein.
