## Prompt

Use case: logo-brand.
Create a professional minimalist vector-style LOGO SYMBOL for Geis, a protein visualization app. This is a graphic identity design task, NOT a protein illustration and NOT an app icon render.
Design idea: distill protein secondary structure into one compact distinctive asymmetric mark: a short stylized alpha helix with only TWO turns flows into ONE folded arrow-ended beta strand. Use broad flat filled shapes and carefully controlled negative-space cuts to suggest a ribbon twisting front to back. Connect the elements into a unified, memorable silhouette. The overall mark is compact, roughly square, with an energetic diagonal flow, neither circular nor a letter. Maximum three simple filled graphic pieces. Use two crisp negative-space slits to communicate the helix. The beta arrow should be integrated as a folded continuation, not a separate navigation arrow. Aim for the visual economy, intentional geometry, and distinctiveness of an excellent software brand symbol designed by a top graphic designer. It should read as a logo in one second and survive as a tiny monochrome favicon.
Palette: exactly one solid deep cobalt blue ink on plain warm white background. Flat 2D, sharp clean vector-like edges, no shading, no gradients, no lighting, no 3D, no texture, no outlines, no shadow, no dark app tile.
Composition: one symbol centered with generous whitespace, mark occupies about 55% of canvas width. No text, wordmark, letters, G, labels, presentation board, mockup, variations, or watermark.
Avoid: realistic molecular structures, many helices, molecular balls, thin loop connector lines, DNA double helix, abstract flower, three petal knot, recycling symbol, generic rounded swirl. Only a highly simplified intelligent protein-derived brand mark.
