Created using the built-in image generator. Artwork: `geis-protein-concept.png`. Preview concept; production icons are unchanged.

## Prompt

Use case: logo-brand.
Asset type: standalone square app icon concept for Geis, a protein structure visualization workbench named after scientific illustrator Irving Geis.
Primary request: a visually unmistakable globular PROTEIN ribbon structure as a beautiful scientific app icon. The previous concept looked like three abstract blue folded petals and was rejected for not looking like a protein. Start fresh: actual recognizable secondary structure is essential.
Subject: a compact asymmetric myoglobin-inspired globular fold comprising five clearly legible alpha helices connected by short thin irregular loops. Each alpha helix must look like a protein ribbon cartoon: a narrow flat ribbon making three or four regular corkscrew turns, with visible alternating front and back faces and visible gaps between turns. Helices arranged in different orientations packing into one coherent globular protein, not a DNA double helix and not a generic swirl. Two helices angled nearly vertically, others diagonally and one across the lower foreground, short connecting loops. Scientifically informed protein-cartoon visual language, simplified to keep clarity at app icon scale. No atom spheres, no beads, no central glowing sphere, no monogram, no letter G, no broad folded petals.
Style: precise exquisite scientific illustration with restrained 3D depth, smooth satin ribbons and excellent occlusion cues, inspired by the care and clarity of classic hand-painted molecular illustrations but clean contemporary execution. Recognizable molecular structure first, logo polish second. Cobalt blue with lighter icy blue facing surfaces and deep blue shaded faces; one short muted teal helix provides subtle orientation contrast. Restrained material with no chrome and no glass.
Composition: single large compact protein centered on a full square deep midnight navy background. Protein occupies 76 percent of width, enough breathing space around it. Orthographic view. Clear silhouette, clean gaps, broad enough ribbons to read at small sizes. Subtle studio illumination, no dramatic effects.
Constraints: single icon artwork only. No text, letters, labels, border, presentation sheet, mockup, DNA, atomic orbital diagram, flowers, trefoils, triangular recycling logo, abstract three-blade knot, sparkles, or watermark. Do not make a scientifically exact claim; this is a deliberately simplified protein-inspired brand mark.
