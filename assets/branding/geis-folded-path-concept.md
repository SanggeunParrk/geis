- `src/styles.css`: ink-dark backgrounds and pale blue accents.
- `README.md`: Geis honors a scientific illustrator who made molecular structure understandable.

## Direction

A folded backbone-like path with over-under crossings expresses spatial structure and connected work. A compact flat symbol paired with the Geis wordmark, rather than a rendering of a protein or a ribbon letter.

## Prompt

Use case: logo-brand.
Design a sophisticated graphic identity logo for "Geis", a desktop molecular creation and exploration workbench. Product research: its core is a node graph connecting prediction, molecular representations, lighting and image creation; its Worlds are navigable molecular spaces; BioMol reveals connections at multiple scales; it is named after scientific illustrator Irving Geis. Its identity should express MAKING SPATIAL STRUCTURE LEGIBLE, not generic biotechnology.
Deliverable: one beautifully composed flat logo lockup, symbol above and the exact word "Geis" below, on a uniform very dark ink-navy background #0b0d12.
Symbol concept: a FOLDED PATH. One continuous bold round-ended line folds back through space into a compact asymmetric three-dimensional cage-like topology, like an elegantly abstracted protein BACKBONE trace or a folded chain drawn by a scientific illustrator. Only SIX or SEVEN rounded angular bends, two clear over/under crossings with small background-colored gaps, and three generous interior negative-space windows. Its outline is compact and roughly hexagonal but irregular, not a regular polygon. The path makes a single coherent distinctive emblem, highly designed and visually balanced. Think a beautifully drawn spatial knot made from a minimal angular skeletal path, with structural logic, neither messy nor decorative. At least one diagonal through its center provides structural tension. Uniform thick stroke, approximately one tenth the symbol width. Open ends neatly terminate inside the composition. It must remain legible in one solid ink at 24px. Use flat pale glacier blue #6cc2ff for the entire symbol, with background-colored crossing gaps doing all depth work. No shading.
Typography: exact text "Geis", capital G lowercase eis. Restrained refined modern grotesk sans serif, medium weight, carefully kerned, pale almost-white #e6e9f2, understated and confident. Symbol width approximately equal to the word width. Generous separation between emblem and word. Strong optical balance and plenty of empty surrounding space.
This must look like a distinctive mature creative scientific SOFTWARE BRAND, not a diagram, protein illustration, flowchart or medical company logo. Do not illustrate actual helices or beta sheets. No ribbon, no letter-shaped symbol, no DNA, no atom balls, no orbitals, no flower, no three-petal trefoil, no arrow, no eye, no generic cube, no gradients, 3D renders, glows, textures, shadows, app tile, mockup, captions or extra words. Single logo lockup only.
